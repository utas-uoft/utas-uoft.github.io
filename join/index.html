Founding team

Seven roles
are open.

Three to five hours a week, starting January. Several of these need no technical background at all, and one of them matters more than any of the others in year one.

01

The numbers

Five is the floor. Seven makes it work.

U of T needs a primary contact, two administrative officers, and five members, and those can overlap. Five people can legally cover all of it, and the application is due in November.

Seven is what it takes to actually run both halves without one person doing everything. The plan is written, the curriculum is written, the site is up and the form is live. What is missing is people willing to put their names on it.

  • 06VP Communications
  • 07Faculty Advisor non-voting
02

What each one does

  1. 01PresidentOwns the direction and the relationship with the University.
  2. 02VP ResearchRuns the pod cycles and enforces the six-week publish rule.
  3. 03VP EducationOwns the Track curriculum. Teaches it, or finds the people who will.
  4. 04VP Events and SpeakersBooks the sector lecture series. No technical background needed, and it matters most in year one.
  5. 05VP OperationsRecognition paperwork, budget, room bookings, student union funding.
  6. 06VP CommunicationsSocials, writeups, the GitHub org, the sponsor one-pager.
  7. 07Faculty AdvisorNon-voting, as University policy requires. Credibility and introductions.
03

  3. Nov 2026Application for University recognition submitted.
  4. Jan 2027Launch. First lecture, first cohort, first research cycle.
